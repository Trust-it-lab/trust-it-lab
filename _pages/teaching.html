Teaching Focus

Dr. Islam's teaching focuses on database management, AI security, generative AI applications, and big data systems, with an emphasis on problem-solving and on designing secure, scalable, data-driven solutions for real-world applications. Across these areas, students develop skills in computational thinking, system design, responsible AI use, and data analysis, and learn to evaluate the opportunities and risks of emerging technologies.

  • Database Management
  • AI Security
  • Generative AI Applications
  • Big Data Systems
